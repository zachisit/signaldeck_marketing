1. What the App Is

Market Posture reports a market-regime reading derived from daily price and volume across a fixed set of publicly traded US exchange-traded funds and index series. The reading is a plain majority of four measured relationships — high-yield credit against its 50-day average, cyclical against defensive sector performance, volatility against its own trend, and small-cap against large-cap performance.

2. What the App Is Not

The App is not investment advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. Specifically:

  • It is not a forecast. Every figure the App displays is backward-looking — a close measured against a prior close, a volume measured against its own average. Nothing in the App predicts the next move of any market.
  • It is not personalised. The reading is identical for every user. It knows nothing about your positions, your account, your risk tolerance, or your objectives, and could not tailor itself to them if it tried.
