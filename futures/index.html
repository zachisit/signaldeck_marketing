Tick Math, Done Right.
Not shares. Not pips. Contracts.

Most journals were built for stock traders. When you log an ES trade, they calculate P&L like equity — wrong units, wrong multiplier, wrong result. SignalDeck uses the actual futures formula: ticks moved × tick value × number of contracts × side − fees. Every product uses its correct contract spec.

ES: $12.50/tick (50 × $0.25). NQ: $5.00/tick (20 × $0.25). CL: $10.00/tick (1,000 × $0.01). GC: $10.00/tick (100 × $0.10). Each product carries its own contract specification, and imported fills are matched to it automatically, micros included.

Add the stop that defined your risk and every trade gets an exact R-multiple. No share-based math, no pip conversions, no phantom R from the wrong multiplier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which plan do I need for futures?

Futures journaling is on the Futures plan, which includes everything in Elite. It is invite-only: tell us how you trade on our Discord and we set the plan up for you. Futures trades you logged before the plan existed stay in your journal on any plan. They are read-only there, and you can still add notes or delete them.

What futures contracts does SignalDeck support?

E-mini S&P 500 (ES) on CME, E-mini Nasdaq-100 (NQ) on CME, Crude Oil (CL) on NYMEX, and Gold (GC) on COMEX. Contract tickers follow the CME monthly convention — ESZ6 for December 2026, for example. Imports also resolve micros (MES, MNQ, MCL, MGC) and other common CME roots with the correct multiplier.

How does P&L calculation work for futures?

Realized P&L = (exit – entry) × side × contract_size × number of contracts − fees. ES: 50 × $0.25/tick = $12.50/tick. NQ: 20 × $0.25/tick = $5.00/tick. CL: 1,000 × $0.01 = $10.00/tick. GC: 100 × $0.10 = $10.00/tick. Each product uses its own contract spec, and imported fills are matched to it automatically.

Can I import futures trades from Tradovate or NinjaTrader?

Yes, on the Futures plan. Personal Tradovate accounts can connect and import on demand. Prop firm accounts on Tradovate (Apex, Topstep and others) have no API access, so upload a Tradovate or NinjaTrader CSV export instead. Fills are rebuilt into round-trip trades with the correct contract multiplier. IBKR imports bring futures rows in too. On other plans, imports skip futures rows and list them, and everything else imports normally.

Does SignalDeck show live prices for open futures positions?

Not currently. Futures trades are journaled from your imported fills plus the stop you set, so realized P&L and R-multiples are exact, but SignalDeck does not stream futures quotes. That means no live unrealized P&L or price alerts on open futures positions.

Does R-Multiple work for futures trading?

Yes. R-Multiple is calculated from your entry, stop, and exit prices — the tick-based P&L is the numerator, your initial risk (in dollars, not ticks) is the denominator. Risk $250 on an ES trade and profit $750 — that is +3R. SQN scoring, expectancy, Kelly Criterion, and the Post Mortem protocol all work identically for futures.

Can I track futures prop firm challenges in SignalDeck?

Yes. Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, and TradaFi all use ES and NQ as their primary instruments. SignalDeck's R-value framework aligns with prop firm risk rules — daily loss limits become natural guardrails when every trade is sized in R. See the Prop Firm page for the full rundown.

Do I need a different journal for CFD vs futures?

No. SignalDeck handles both in the same platform. If you trade NAS100 (CFD) and NQ (futures), or XAUUSD (CFD) and GC (futures), both are logged in the same journal with the correct math for each instrument type. CFD trades use broker-native tickers; futures trades (on the Futures plan) use CME-convention tickers with tick-aligned P&L. See the CFD page for more.

What happens when my futures contract expires?

Each trade is permanently bound to its contract ticker (e.g., ESZ6), and it stays in your journal with its recorded history intact after that contract expires. Trades in the next contract month import under that contract's ticker (e.g., ESH7). Positions are not rolled automatically.

Are micro futures (MES, MNQ) supported?

Yes, through import. Tradovate, NinjaTrader and IBKR imports resolve MES, MNQ, MCL and MGC, plus other common CME roots such as YM, RTY, ZB and ZN, with the correct multiplier. Spread and combo contracts are not supported; multi-leg spreads import as separate legs.
