Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use R-Multiple tracking for crypto trading?

Yes. R-Multiple works identically for crypto. If you risk $200 on a BTC trade and make $600, that is a 3R trade regardless of the asset. In fact, crypto's extreme volatility makes disciplined R-tracking even more important — a single oversized loss can erase weeks of gains without a risk-normalized framework.

Does SignalDeck support 24/7 crypto trading hours?

Yes. There are no market-hours restrictions — log a Saturday BTC breakout, a Sunday ETH reversal, or a 3AM altcoin scalp at any time. The calendar view shows your full 7-day trading week, and day-of-week and time-of-day filters work across the full 24/7 schedule so you can find your real edge window.

Can I track trades from multiple exchanges in one journal?

Yes. Each trade can be tagged with an exchange (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, and others). You can then filter your dashboard, SQN score, and R-multiple analytics by exchange to see which venue produces the best fills and where your edge is strongest.

Can I journal leveraged crypto positions and fractional coin amounts?

Yes. SignalDeck logs both spot and leveraged positions in R-terms. Enter fractional coin amounts, tag the exchange, and log your leverage factor alongside the entry. Leverage multiplies P&L but doesn't change the risk framework — the 1R you define before entry is what your analytics measure.
