Permissions & safety

You decide what it can touch.

Three permission levels, chosen when you connect. The defaults are conservative, and nothing reaches your broker.

Read

Default

Trades, stats, strategies, journal, calendar, goals, backtests, live runs and pre-trade checks. Nothing changes.

Write

Opt in

Open, edit and close trades. Add notes, tags, signals, checklists, post-mortems, alerts, screenshots and journal entries. The assistant confirms each change with you first.

Delete

Separate opt in

Off unless you tick it on top of write. Deleting a trade also means typing back its symbol, because a deleted trade can't be restored.

Journal only. No broker orders, no billing, no account settings.
Your account only. A connection can never see or change another user's data.
Automated book is off limits. The scanner's managed positions can't be changed.
Hourly write limits. A runaway assistant can't rewrite your journal in bulk.
Revoke in one click. Settings → AI assistants (MCP) lists every token and connected app.
Password change cuts everything. Resetting or changing your password revokes every connection.
Imported trades warn. Editing a broker-synced field tells you the next re-import will overwrite it.
Standard OAuth sign-in. PKCE, short-lived access tokens and a consent screen that shows exactly what you're granting.

Setup

Connect in about two minutes.

You need a SignalDeck account (the free plan works). Then pick your assistant.

Server URL https://app.sgnldk.com/api/mcp

Claude & ChatGPT

claude.ai · ChatGPT · sign-in flow

  1. In your assistant's settings, add a custom connector and paste the server URL.
  2. Sign in to SignalDeck when prompted.
  3. On the consent screen, choose read, write and (optionally) delete, then Approve.

Custom connectors may need a paid assistant plan or developer mode, depending on the provider.

Claude Code

terminal · personal access token

  1. In SignalDeck: Settings → AI assistants (MCP). Create a token and tick write or delete if you want them.
  2. Run:
claude mcp add --transport http \
  signaldeck https://app.sgnldk.com/api/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer sd_pat_…"

Ask it to call whoami to confirm the account.

Claude Desktop

desktop app · token or connector

  1. Create a token in Settings → AI assistants (MCP).
  2. Copy the ready-made Claude Desktop config snippet shown next to your new token into Claude Desktop's config.
  3. Restart Claude Desktop. SignalDeck appears in your tools.

Tokens are shown once. Revoke them any time from the same screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI assistant call tools in another app. SignalDeck runs an MCP server, so a connected assistant can read your journal and, if you allow it, make changes to it on your behalf.

Which AI assistants work with SignalDeck?

Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code) and ChatGPT. claude.ai and ChatGPT connect with a sign-in and approval screen. Claude Code and Claude Desktop can also use a personal access token from Settings. Any MCP client that supports remote HTTP servers with OAuth or bearer tokens can connect the same way.

Can the assistant change or delete my trades?

Only if you allow it. Read access is the default. Write access is a separate permission you grant when you connect, and the assistant is instructed to show you each change and get your confirmation first. Deleting is a further opt-in on top of write, and deleting a trade also requires confirming its symbol, because a deleted trade can't be restored.

Can it place trades at my broker?

No. The assistant works inside your SignalDeck journal only. It never touches your broker, billing or account settings, and it can never change the automated scanner's managed positions.

Which plan includes it?

Every plan, including Free. Your plan's normal limits still apply through the assistant. For example, Free allows one screenshot per trade, and premium analytics fields stay premium. See plans →

What if I edit a trade that was imported from my broker?

The change is made, and the assistant tells you which fields came from the broker sync. The next forced re-import from MT5, cTrader, SnapTrade, IBKR or Tradovate will overwrite those fields. Manual and CSV trades never warn.

How do I disconnect an assistant?

In SignalDeck, go to Settings → AI assistants (MCP). Revoke a personal access token, or disconnect an app under Connected apps, and access stops immediately. Changing or resetting your password also cuts off every existing connection. Logging out of the web app does not.
