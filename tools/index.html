Frequently Asked Questions

Are these trading calculators free?

Yes — every calculator here is free, runs instantly in your browser, and needs no account or signup. The SignalDeck journal is optional — use it if you want the same metrics calculated from your real trades.

Which trading calculator should I start with?

Start with the Position Size Calculator, because correct sizing controls how much any single trade can cost you. Then use the R-Multiple and Expectancy calculators to measure results in R, and the Drawdown Recovery and Kelly Criterion calculators to check whether your risk per trade is sustainable.

What is the R-value framework?

The R-value framework measures every trade as a multiple of the amount risked, where 1R is the distance from entry to stop. Expressing results in R makes trades of different sizes and instruments comparable, and it is the unit SignalDeck uses for expectancy, SQN and position sizing.
